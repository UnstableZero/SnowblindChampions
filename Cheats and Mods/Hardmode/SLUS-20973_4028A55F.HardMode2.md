I guess I can consider this project started now. No timeline, no expectations of finishing, I do this when it's fun.

1. Adjust the math instead of character stats, allowing for Vanilla characters to be imported.
  - Remove the internal HP/MP penalty, then balance.
  - Reduce the attack bonus from Strength and Dexterity.
  - Refactor HP/MP Regeneration.
  - Strength maxxing must remain viable, even if punishing.
  - Stamina will feel like a "must" for many people.

1a. Now that the math for creature setup is discovered, it's the main target.
  - Experience is affected by world difficulty, and therefore, difficulty changes.
  - This problem is solved by scaling the experience reward math. (Especially for multiplayer.)
  - Creatures feel weak part way through the game, and especially in later difficulties.
  - This can be tackled by changing the math to scale armor, attack, and damage more aggressively, and relative to difficulty.
  - It is important to not turn the enemy into a damage sponge.
  - By emphasing enemy armor more in multiplayer, we can even out the value of multiple melee players, and casters.
  - Player armor eventually scales ahead of enemy attack, and CQC becomes less dangerous.
  - Enemies will get higher attack values to punch through player armor a bit more often at higher levels.
  - Minibosses don't scale that well, we can change that too.

2. Better World Difficulty scaling, adjusting the starting values, and plane multiplier.
  - Increase the starting World Difficulty, of each Game Difficulty.
  - Increase the multiplier, so the game scales appropriately in each playthrough.
  - Ensure Boss HP doesn't get out of hand.
  - Don't kill the player too much.

2a. Difficulty scaling matters less now with control over creature setup math.
  - The multiplier for each GameDifficulty still matters.
  - Starting values for later difficulties still need adjustment, just less.

3. Rebalance figurines, bears are simply too powerful.
  - The carry weight of Perfect Bear Figurines should be increased to prevent the ridiculous stacking potential. (300+)
  - Other Figurines will likely get the same treatment to a lesser extent.

4. Attempt to improve weak abilties, allowing for more variety.
  - Some abilities would be great staples of combat, if they didn't suck.
  - Shield Bash and Ground Pound are terrible in comparison to Slam and Multifire.
  - Harm Touch ought to provide more value for the mana and time cost.
  - Nerf the overpowered abilities. (Slam, Multifire, Dual Wield, Strong Stance, Natural Resistance)
  - Compare the effects of Ancestral Call and Battle Cry.

5. Spells need serious work, and a lot of math.
  - Spell data can be manipulated quite readily.
  - Custom sub-routines would allow for more math, and damage.
  - Wizard, Cleric, and Shadow Knight should see some reward for their high Intelligence.

6. Multiplayer games need something extra to reduce the impact of a group.
  - More aggressive difficulty starts and scaling.
  - Research the efficacy of blocking, and whether regular enemies could break a block.

6a. Creatures will be more threatening in multiplayer.
  - More players means more weapon swings, and spells cast.
  - Enemy armor will be increased to prevent easy bum-rash tactics.
  - Enemy health will see a minor bump, sponges aren't fun.
  - Experience rewards will be reduced, a larger party just has such a MASSIVE power advantage.
